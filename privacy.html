Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 4, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to The 3AM Channel ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a safe, supportive space during the quiet hours of the night.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our service.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Post Content: The text content you choose to share
  • User ID: A randomly generated identifier stored locally on your device
  • Anonymity Preference: Your choice to post anonymously or not

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Timezone Data: To ensure proper time window enforcement
  • Connection Data: IP address, browser type (for security and analytics)
  • Usage Data: Which features you use, when you visit

2.3 Technical Data

  • Logs: Server logs for security and debugging purposes
  • Cookies: Essential cookies for functionality (no tracking cookies)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and maintain the service
  • Enable real-time communication features
  • Enforce time-window restrictions
  • Implement the daily retention policy (keep top post)
  • Prevent abuse and ensure user safety
  • Analyze usage patterns to improve the service
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Data Retention

Smart Retention Policy:

  • Posts from each day are deleted at 4:05 AM the following day
  • Only the single most-reacted post from each day is retained
  • Kept posts remain visible but are anonymized after 7 days
  • Server logs are retained for 14 days for security purposes

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do NOT sell your personal information. We may share information only in these limited circumstances:

  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect rights and safety
  • Service Providers: MongoDB Atlas, hosting provider (with data processing agreements)
  • With Your Consent: Any other disclosure will require your explicit consent

We do NOT share with:

  • Advertisers or marketing companies
  • Data brokers
  • Social media platforms

  • Access: Request a copy of your data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your posts (before daily cleanup)

  • Opt-Out: Stop using the service at any time
